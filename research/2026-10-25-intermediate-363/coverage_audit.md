## Remaining gaps

Many countries and small institutional allocations have not received a complete independent review. The country checklist explicitly distinguishes a positive record from absence of verification. International network membership alone does not establish a domestic facility or current node availability.

Solar, gamma-ray, neutrino/cosmic-ray and specialized facilities are unevenly covered. There is no completed systematic neutrino/cosmic-ray access review. University facilities, restricted national programmes, Africa, Central America and the Caribbean, Pacific islands, parts of Central/Southeast/Western Asia and non-English institutional portals need further passes. Some official pages required login, timed out, returned 403/451 or exposed only old calls.

Detailed manuals, all instruments, exact current configurations, fee schedules and eligibility clauses were not fully extracted for every row. Sources supporting access are present, but that does not imply every requested technical field is verified. Instrument availability can differ from the general facility list.
